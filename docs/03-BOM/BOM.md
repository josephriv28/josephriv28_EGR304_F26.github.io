## Overview
For this individual subsystem to function properly, a wide array of parts and materials are required. Many of these parts do not require purchase as they can found in Peralta 109, located at Arizona State University's Polytechnic Campus. Some parts in particular that requires online purchase would include the MCP9700-E/TO, 0251.500MXL fuses, and at least ten 5010 Red PC testing points, for the possibility that one of the parts go missing, start to malfunction, or end up in any other undesirable situation.
