## Overview
For this individual subsystem to function properly, a wide array of parts and materials are required. Many of these parts do not require purchase as they can found in Peralta 109, located at Arizona State University's Polytechnic Campus. One part in particular that requires online purchase would be the MCP9700-E/TO, which was ordered in a quantity of 5, for the possibility that one of the sensors go missing, start to malfunction, or end up in any other undesirable situation.
